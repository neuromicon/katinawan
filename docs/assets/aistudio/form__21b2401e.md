# Sacred Plant Spirit Healing
## Google Forms Application & Registration Specification

**Document type:** Form creation specification  
**Platform:** Google Forms + Google Sheets  
**Language:** English  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Ceremony applications, preliminary safety screening, and registration management

---

## 1. Purpose

The Google Form will serve as the primary application channel for Sacred Plant Spirit Healing ceremonies.

It must allow prospective participants to:

- Submit their contact information.
- Select a preferred ceremony date from confirmed dates.
- Describe their intentions and relevant previous experience.
- Disclose health and safety considerations.
- Provide an emergency contact.
- Review the privacy notice and provide informed consent for application processing.

The linked Google Sheet will store submitted responses and support the organizer's application review process.

**Important:** Submitting the form does not confirm acceptance, reserve a place, or establish that participation is medically safe. All applications require manual review.

## 2. Platform and Workflow

**Components**

1. Google Forms — public application interface.
2. Google Sheets — private response database and administrative tracker.
3. Organizer email — application notifications and direct communication.
4. Manual review — acceptance, follow-up, deposit instructions, and booking confirmation.

**Application workflow**

1. Applicant submits the form.
2. Responses are recorded in Google Sheets.
3. Organizer reviews the application.
4. Organizer contacts the applicant if clarification is required.
5. Organizer approves, declines, or waitlists the application.
6. For approved applications, the organizer provides the confirmed deposit instructions.
7. The organizer manually verifies the deposit.
8. The organizer sends the final booking confirmation.

The form must not automatically promise acceptance or confirmed availability.

**Section description**

The following questions help identify health and safety topics that may require a private follow-up before your application can be reviewed.

Please answer honestly. These questions do not diagnose medical conditions or establish that participation is medically safe.

If you are uncertain about a health concern or a medication interaction, consult a qualified healthcare professional. Do not stop or change prescribed medication for a ceremony.

### Question 10. Relevant health history

- Type: Checkboxes
- Required: Yes

**Label:** Do any of the following apply to you now or in your medical history?

**Options:**
- Heart or cardiovascular condition, irregular heartbeat, previous heart attack or stroke
- High or uncontrolled blood pressure
- Seizures or epilepsy
- Bipolar disorder, psychosis, schizophrenia, or a history of psychotic episodes
- Another mental-health condition or significant psychological crisis that may be relevant
- Recent suicidal thoughts or a recent psychiatric emergency
- Pregnant, possibly pregnant, or breastfeeding
- Significant medical condition, recent surgery, or a condition affecting mobility or consciousness
- Known allergy or previous serious allergic reaction
- None of these apply
- I prefer to discuss health information privately

**Help text:** Select all that apply. If you select “None of these apply” together with another option, the organizer may ask you to clarify.

**Implementation note:** Google Forms checkboxes do not inherently make “None of these apply” mutually exclusive with other choices. The organizer must review contradictory answers manually.

## 10. Google Sheets Structure

Google Forms should create the response spreadsheet automatically. Do not manually rearrange or rename the generated response columns without checking their relationship to the form.

Create an additional worksheet named **Admin Tracker** with these columns:

| Column | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Application date | Date received |
| Applicant name | Applicant identification |
| Email | Communication |
| Preferred date | Scheduling |
| Review status | Current application stage |
| Health follow-up | Yes / No / Pending |

| Admin notes | Restricted operational notes |

### Review status options

- New
- Under review
- Follow-up needed
- Approved — deposit requested
- Deposit reported
- Confirmed
- Waitlist
- Declined
- Cancelled

### Deposit status options

- Not requested
- Requested
- Reported
- Verified
- Refunded
- Not applicable

The tracker should contain only the operational information necessary for booking management. Detailed health disclosures should remain in the restricted form response data and should not be copied into general administrative notes.

## 11. Privacy and Access Controls

Health-related information is sensitive. Apply the following minimum controls:

1. Store the spreadsheet in the organizer's Google account.
2. Restrict spreadsheet access to named, authorized people.
3. Do not enable public access to the response spreadsheet.
4. Do not publish response data, screenshots, or individual health answers.
5. Use a separate public form link for applicants.
6. Avoid collecting unnecessary personal or medical information.
7. Define a data-retention period and a deletion procedure.
8. Verify the organizer's obligations under applicable privacy law before launch.
9. Do not use health responses for unrelated marketing.
10. If a health concern requires follow-up, discuss it privately and limit access to those who need the information.

Google Forms and Sheets provide practical collection and recordkeeping, but they do not replace an appropriate health assessment, emergency plan, or legal review.
